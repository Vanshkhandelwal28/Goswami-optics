# Goswami Optics Kota — Editorial Eyewear Storefront

A responsive, client-side storefront for **Goswami Optics, Kota, Rajasthan**. The visual direction pairs warm editorial photography and restrained serif typography with practical optical-store information.

## Highlights

- **Responsive editorial storefront** with a warm stone, espresso and olive palette, Cormorant Garamond display type and DM Sans interface type.
- **Photo-first product browsing** with curated local lifestyle imagery, color finishes, category filters, magnetic clip-on details and a product photo gallery. The photographs are styling references; exact product and color availability can be confirmed in-store.
- **Short lookbook film** assembled from the existing local editorial photographs, with an explicit play control and no autoplay.
- **Lens customization and reservation** for prescription, zero-power, progressive, anti-glare, blue-cut and photochromic options. Reservations open WhatsApp; there is no online checkout.
- **Shortlist and favorites** saved locally in the browser, with a WhatsApp handoff and no account required.
- **Frame finder quiz** with recommendations based on face shape, use, silhouette and finish.
- **Authorised brand showcase** with bespoke brand styling and smooth, infinite marquee motion.
- **Two Kota showroom profiles**, Google Maps directions, phone and WhatsApp contact, plus local visitor reviews and review helpful-votes.
- **No backend, database, sign-in or sign-up flows.**
